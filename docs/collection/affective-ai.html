How to pronounce affective AI terms

Persona, affect, valence, arousal, interoception, prosody, and other emotion-aware AI terms with audio, IPA, and sources.

Emotion-aware agents combine vocabulary from psychology, speech, neuroscience, and embodied cognition. Familiar-looking words such as affect and valence change meaning or stress in technical use, while interoception and autopoiesis are easy to misread.

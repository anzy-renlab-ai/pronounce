How to pronounce Objective-C

product community-consensus

objective C /əbˈdʒɛktɪv ˈsiː/ mp3

Objective-C is most commonly pronounced "objective C" (/əbˈdʒɛktɪv ˈsiː/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

The C is the letter: 'objective see', /əbˈdʒɛktɪv siː/. Stress sits on the second syllable of 'objective'.

Pronouncing project and product names correctly avoids the small but persistent friction of being gently corrected during standups, conference Q&As, and team calls. How do you pronounce Objective-C?

Objective-C is pronounced "objective C" (/əbˈdʒɛktɪv ˈsiː/). The C is the letter: 'objective see', /əbˈdʒɛktɪv siː/. Stress sits on the second syllable of 'objective'.

What is the IPA for Objective-C?

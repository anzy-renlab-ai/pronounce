How to pronounce ajax

acronym community-consensus

AY jaks /ˈeɪdʒæks/ mp3

ajax is most commonly pronounced "AY jaks" (/ˈeɪdʒæks/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

Word-read AY-jaks per the Wikipedia lead; coined 2005 for Asynchronous JavaScript and XML — the all-caps AJAX is the acronym, said as a word.

How do you pronounce ajax?

ajax is pronounced "AY jaks" (/ˈeɪdʒæks/). Word-read AY-jaks per the Wikipedia lead; coined 2005 for Asynchronous JavaScript and XML — the all-caps AJAX is the acronym, said as a word. Source: Wikipedia lead (IPAc-en).

What is the IPA for ajax?

The IPA for ajax is /ˈeɪdʒæks/, respelled "AY jaks".

How to pronounce Node.js

product community-consensus

node J S /noʊd ˌdʒeɪ ˈɛs/ mp3

Node.js is most commonly pronounced "node J S" (/noʊd ˌdʒeɪ ˈɛs/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

The extension is spoken: 'node jay-ess', /noʊd dʒeɪ ɛs/ — not 'node dot js' and not 'node-jays'. Everyone shortens it to just 'node' in speech.

Pronouncing project and product names correctly avoids the small but persistent friction of being gently corrected during standups, conference Q&As, and team calls. How do you pronounce Node.js?

Node.js is pronounced "node J S" (/noʊd ˌdʒeɪ ˈɛs/). The extension is spoken: 'node jay-ess', /noʊd dʒeɪ ɛs/ — not 'node dot js' and not 'node-jays'. Everyone shortens it to just 'node' in speech.

What is the IPA for Node.js?

The IPA for Node.js is /noʊd ˌdʒeɪ ˈɛs/, respelled "node J S".

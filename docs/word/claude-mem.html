How to pronounce claude-mem

project community-consensus

clawed MEM /ˌklɔːd ˈmɛm/ mp3

claude-mem is most commonly pronounced "clawed MEM" (/ˌklɔːd ˈmɛm/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

Two spoken words: "clawed MEM". 'mem' said as a word (like Memcached, mem0), not spelled M-E-M. Persistent-memory plugin for coding agents; the creator's naming doc fixes "Claude-Mem" as the prose form.

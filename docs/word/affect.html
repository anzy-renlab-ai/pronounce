How to pronounce affect

cs-term community-consensus

AFF ekt /ˈæfɛkt/ mp3
or: uh FEKT /əˈfɛkt/

affect is most commonly pronounced "AFF ekt" (/ˈæfɛkt/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

In affective computing and psychology, the noun meaning observable emotion or feeling is AFF-ect, with first-syllable stress. The everyday verb meaning "influence" is uh-FECT; both established readings are included because the spelling is identical.

Alternate readings you might hear:

  • "uh FEKT" /əˈfɛkt/

Source: Cambridge Dictionary — affect (noun and verb)

How to pronounce Le Chonk

product community-consensus

luh chongk /lə ˈtʃɑŋk/ mp3
or: lee chongk /ˈliː ˈtʃɑŋk/

Le Chonk is most commonly pronounced "luh chongk" (/lə ˈtʃɑŋk/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

Mistral's own nickname for Mistral Large 4 (1T params): "Unofficially ML4, very officially: le Chonk." Le as in the sibling product Le Chat (luh), matching the meme spelling chonk.

Alternate readings you might hear:

  • "lee chongk" /ˈliː ˈtʃɑŋk/

Source: Mistral official announcement

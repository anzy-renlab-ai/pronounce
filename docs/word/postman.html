How to pronounce Postman

product community-consensus

post man /ˈpoʊstˌmən/ mp3

Postman is most commonly pronounced "post man" (/ˈpoʊstˌmən/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

API platform named for the mail carrier; ordinary English compound POHST-man.

Source: Postman official site

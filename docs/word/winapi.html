How to pronounce winapi

cs-term community-consensus

win A P I /wɪn ˌeɪ piː ˈaɪ/ mp3

winapi is most commonly pronounced "win A P I" (/wɪn ˌeɪ piː ˈaɪ/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

'win ay-pee-eye': the word 'win', then three letters. Never 'why-nappy'.

How do you pronounce winapi?

winapi is pronounced "win A P I" (/wɪn ˌeɪ piː ˈaɪ/). 'win ay-pee-eye': the word 'win', then three letters. Never 'why-nappy'.

What is the IPA for winapi?

The IPA for winapi is /wɪn ˌeɪ piː ˈaɪ/, respelled "win A P I".

How to pronounce Neo Lab

project community-consensus

NEE oh lab /ˈniːoʊ læb/ mp3

Neo Lab is most commonly pronounced "NEE oh lab" (/ˈniːoʊ læb/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

RenLab's NEO LAB company-profile series. This entry reads the title compositionally as the US prefix "NEE-oh" plus standard English "lab"; no creator pronunciation guide was found, so it remains community-consensus.

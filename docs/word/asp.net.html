How to pronounce ASP.NET

product community-consensus

A S P dot net /ˌeɪ ɛs ˈpiː dɑːt nɛt/ mp3

ASP.NET is most commonly pronounced "A S P dot net" (/ˌeɪ ɛs ˈpiː dɑːt nɛt/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

Letters, then the framework: 'ay-ess-pee dot net'. The dot is part of the name and always spoken.

How do you pronounce ASP.NET?

ASP.NET is pronounced "A S P dot net" (/ˌeɪ ɛs ˈpiː dɑːt nɛt/). Letters, then the framework: 'ay-ess-pee dot net'. The dot is part of the name and always spoken.

What is the IPA for ASP.NET?

The IPA for ASP.NET is /ˌeɪ ɛs ˈpiː dɑːt nɛt/, respelled "A S P dot net".

How to pronounce NVFP4

acronym community-consensus

N V F P four /ˌɛn viː ɛf piː ˈfɔr/ mp3

NVFP4 is most commonly pronounced "N V F P four" (/ˌɛn viː ɛf piː ˈfɔr/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

NVIDIA's 4-bit floating-point format (E2M1 values, E4M3 block scales) on Blackwell/Rubin; read letter-by-letter like other precision initialisms.

Source: NVIDIA Technical Blog — NVFP4

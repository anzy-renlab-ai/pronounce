How to pronounce interoception

cs-term community-consensus

in ter oh SEP shun /ˌɪntəroʊˈsɛpʃən/ mp3

interoception is most commonly pronounced "in ter oh SEP shun" (/ˌɪntəroʊˈsɛpʃən/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

The sensing of internal bodily signals such as heartbeat, breathing, and hunger; increasingly used in embodied-agent and computational-emotion models. Say in-ter-oh-SEP-shun.

How do you pronounce interoception?

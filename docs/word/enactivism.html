How to pronounce enactivism

cs-term community-consensus

ih NAK tih viz um /ɪˈnæktɪvɪzəm/ mp3

enactivism is most commonly pronounced "ih NAK tih viz um" (/ɪˈnæktɪvɪzəm/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

A cognitive-science view in which cognition emerges through an agent's embodied interaction with its environment rather than passive representation alone. Say ih-NAK-tiv-iz-um.

Source: Wikipedia — Enactivism

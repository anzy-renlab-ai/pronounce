How to pronounce OLED

acronym community-consensus

OH led /ˈoʊˌlɛd/ mp3
or: oh el ee DEE /ˌoʊˌɛlˌiːˈdiː/

OLED is most commonly pronounced "OH led" (/ˈoʊˌlɛd/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

Two live readings: word-read OH-led (Cambridge US primary) and letter-read O-L-E-D; Cambridge US lists both.

Alternate readings you might hear:

  • "oh el ee DEE" /ˌoʊˌɛlˌiːˈdiː/

Source: Cambridge Dictionary (US) — both readings stated

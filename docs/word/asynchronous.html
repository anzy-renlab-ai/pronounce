How to pronounce asynchronous

cs-term community-consensus

ay SING kruh nuhs /eɪˈsɪŋkrənəs/ mp3

asynchronous is most commonly pronounced "ay SING kruh nuhs" (/eɪˈsɪŋkrənəs/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

Stress on the second syllable: ay-SING-kruh-nuhs; the short form async already has its own entry.

Source: Cambridge Dictionary (US)

How to pronounce 4-bitter Lesson

cs-term creator-clarified

four bitter lesson /ˌfɔːrˈbɪtər ˈlɛsən/ mp3

4-bitter Lesson is most commonly pronounced "four bitter lesson" (/ˌfɔːrˈbɪtər ˈlɛsən/). This pronunciation is documented by the project itself (see humans& — The 4-bitter Lesson).

humans& pun on Sutton's Bitter Lesson for its 4-bit RL recipe; reads four-BIT-ter LESS-un.

Source: humans& — The 4-bitter Lesson

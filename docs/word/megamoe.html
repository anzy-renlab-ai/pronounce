How to pronounce MegaMoE

cs-term contested

MEG uh em oh EE /ˈmɛɡə ˌɛm oʊ ˈiː/ mp3
or: MEG uh moh /ˈmɛɡə moʊ/

MegaMoE is most commonly pronounced "MEG uh em oh EE" (/ˈmɛɡə ˌɛm oʊ ˈiː/). This is one of several readings in active use; the developer community hasn't converged on a single answer.

Distributed MoE decode kernel from humans& (in FlashInfer and SGLang); mega + MoE, inheriting MoE's spelled-out vs "moh" split.

Alternate readings you might hear:

  • "MEG uh moh" /ˈmɛɡə moʊ/

How do you pronounce MegaMoE?

MegaMoE is pronounced "MEG uh em oh EE" (/ˈmɛɡə ˌɛm oʊ ˈiː/). Distributed MoE decode kernel from humans& (in FlashInfer and SGLang); mega + MoE, inheriting MoE's spelled-out vs "moh" split. Source: humans& blog — Faster-than-light NVFP4 decode.

What is the IPA for MegaMoE?

Is MegaMoE sometimes pronounced "MEG uh moh"?

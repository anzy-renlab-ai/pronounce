How to pronounce Eclipse

product contested

ih clips /ɪˈklɪps/ mp3
or: ee clips /iːˈklɪps/

Eclipse is most commonly pronounced "ih clips" (/ɪˈklɪps/). This is one of several readings in active use; the developer community hasn't converged on a single answer.

The IDE splits the community: 'ih-CLIPS' /ɪˈklɪps/, like the astronomical event, against 'ee-CLIPS' /iːˈklɪps/, which the Foundation's own presenters use. Both are heard at every Java conference.

Alternate readings you might hear:

  • "ee clips" /iːˈklɪps/

How do you pronounce Eclipse?

Eclipse is pronounced "ih clips" (/ɪˈklɪps/). The IDE splits the community: 'ih-CLIPS' /ɪˈklɪps/, like the astronomical event, against 'ee-CLIPS' /iːˈklɪps/, which the Foundation's own presenters use. Both are heard at every Java conference.

What is the IPA for Eclipse?

The IPA for Eclipse is /ɪˈklɪps/, respelled "ih clips".

Is Eclipse sometimes pronounced "ee clips"?

Yes — "ee clips" (/iːˈklɪps/) is one of the alternate readings of Eclipse in active use.

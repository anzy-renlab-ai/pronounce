How to pronounce affective

cs-term community-consensus

uh FEK tiv /əˈfɛktɪv/ mp3

affective is most commonly pronounced "uh FEK tiv" (/əˈfɛktɪv/). This is the widely-used reading among engineers, though edge cases exist.

Affective computing and affective AI model emotion, mood, and emotionally responsive interaction. Say uh-FEK-tiv; it is often confused with "effective."

Source: Cambridge Dictionary — affective (US /əˈfek.tɪv/)

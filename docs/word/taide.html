How to pronounce TAIDE

project creator-clarified

tie duh /ˈtaɪ də/ mp3

TAIDE is most commonly pronounced "tie duh" (/ˈtaɪ də/). This pronunciation is documented by the project itself (see Tsai Tzong-Han @ TFC (43:05)).

TAIDE model-team convener Tsai Tzong-Han compares and repeats the Taiwanese Mandarin shorthand 台德/泰德, approximately "TIE-duh" in General American; do not spell out T-A-I-D-E.

Source: Tsai Tzong-Han @ TFC (43:05)

How do you pronounce TAIDE?

TAIDE is pronounced "tie duh" (/ˈtaɪ də/). TAIDE model-team convener Tsai Tzong-Han compares and repeats the Taiwanese Mandarin shorthand 台德/泰德, approximately "TIE-duh" in General American; do not spell out T-A-I-D-E. Source: Tsai Tzong-Han @ TFC (43:05).

What is the IPA for TAIDE?

The IPA for TAIDE is /ˈtaɪ də/, respelled "tie duh".

How to pronounce T-SQL

acronym contested

T sequel /ˌtiː ˈsiːkwəl/ mp3
or: T S Q L /ˌtiː ˌɛs kjuː ˈɛl/

T-SQL is most commonly pronounced "T sequel" (/ˌtiː ˈsiːkwəl/). This is one of several readings in active use; the developer community hasn't converged on a single answer.

Microsoft's dialect: 'tee-sequel' is the common reading, but plenty of DBAs say the letters 'tee-ess-kyoo-ell'. Both are in circulation inside Microsoft's own docs and talks.

Alternate readings you might hear:

  • "T S Q L" /ˌtiː ˌɛs kjuː ˈɛl/
